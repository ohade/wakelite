WakeLite Logo Concepts

A lighthouse shines through the dark, guiding ships to safe harbor. WakeLite watches through sleep, guiding your tasks to safe completion — always on, reliability-first.

Concept 1

The Beacon

A classic lighthouse on rocky shores, dual light beams sweeping across the night sky. The lantern room glows amber through glass panels while the indigo tower stands firm against the dark ocean. Red navigation stripes mark the tower — a trusted, visible sentinel.

Tower gradient C7D2FE to 6366F1 with two red stripes at 60% opacity. Lantern room has 4 individual glass panels with amber gradient. Dual sweeping beam cones (right at 60%, left at 35%) using radial gradient fill. Triple-layer lantern glow (r=16/10/5). Rocky base with irregular polygon. Two ocean wave paths. Eight ambient stars.

WakeLite

Indigo tower, amber lantern, red stripes, deep ocean night

Concept 2

Night Watch

Minimal and geometric — a clean trapezoid tower, a single powerful beam cutting through darkness, and a crescent moon. Stripped to essentials: the light source, the direction, the night. This is WakeLite as a quiet sentinel. It doesn't shout, it just watches — and when the time comes, it shines.

Three-stop horizontal gradient on tower (818CF8 to A5B4FC to 6366F1). Concentric lantern circles: 14px border, 9px glow, 5px core, 2.5px hot center. Single right-facing beam with solid polygon + blurred polygon + center line. Crescent moon via overlapping circles. Dashed water reflection lines. No ornament — pure geometry.

WakeLite

Silver-indigo geometry, golden beam, moonlit night

Concept 3

Storm Keeper

The lighthouse stands firm on a cliff while a storm rages — clouds, rain, rough seas, even lightning. But the beam cuts through regardless. This is WakeLite's promise: your timers fire no matter what. Crash recovery, WAL journaling, launchd KeepAlive — the light stays on through every storm.

Five storm cloud ellipses at varying opacity. Six angled rain lines. Rough sea with filled wave polygon + stroke wave path. Jagged cliff with two-layer rocks. Three red tower stripes. Dual beams with both soft-glow and sharp-edge polygons. Lightning polyline in sky. Central glow persists through storm haze at low opacity — the signal is weakened but never extinguished.

WakeLite

White-indigo tower, amber beam through storm, red stripes

Concept 4

Harbor Light

Warm and inviting — the lighthouse glows softly over calm waters, its light reflecting on the harbor surface. A small boat is moored at a pier, safely home. No sweeping beam — instead, an omnidirectional warmth. This is WakeLite as the place your tasks come home to. The safe harbor after the schedule runs.

White-to-lavender tower gradient (F9FAFB to A5B4FC). Four lantern panels alternating FBBF24/FDE68A for warmth. Omnidirectional glow in three layers (r=30/18/8). Water reflection: ellipse + three dashed lines at decreasing opacity. Pier with two posts and a tiny boat silhouette. Round cross-pane window. Arched door. Three-stop sky gradient (night to deep indigo).

WakeLite

White tower, warm amber glow, calm harbor night

Concept 5

Signal Tower

The lighthouse reimagined as a digital signal tower — circuit traces radiate from the base to connected timer nodes. A hexagonal lantern room pulses with amber light, sending signal rings outward. Active nodes glow gold (connected timers), dormant ones stay indigo. This is WakeLite as infrastructure: a central control tower in a network of scheduled operations.

Eight symmetric circuit traces with polylines. Eight endpoint nodes (three active in amber with neon glow, five dormant in indigo). Hexagonal lantern room (outer + inner hex). Triple concentric signal rings at 18%/12%/8%. Tower has four horizontal LED strip accents. Antenna finial with two crossbars. Core light: three concentric circles (FBBF24 > FDE68A > FFF7ED). Neon glow filter on tower and active nodes.

WakeLite

Indigo circuits, amber signal nodes, hexagonal tech
